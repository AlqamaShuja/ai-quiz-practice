“Sab se lamba option = sahi jawab” — yahan yeh trick fail hoti hai

Aapka observation sahi hai: is quiz ke 100 sawalon mein se 72 (72%) mein sahi jawab wahi option hai jo sab se lamba hai. Lekin 28 sawalon mein aisa nahi — aur un mein se 12 mein sahi jawab sab se chhota option hai. Yeh page sirf wohi 28 sawal dikhata hai, taake aap length ki aadat na dalein. Har option ke saamne uski length (characters) likhi hai, aur sab se lamba option amber border se mark hai.

72%Longest = correct
28Exceptions (yahan)
12Correct = shortest
16Correct = middle
Imtihaan ke liye nasihat: length ka pattern is PDF ke likhne ke andaz ki wajah se hai (sahi jawab ke sath aksar wazahat likh di gayi hai) — yeh AI ka koi qanoon nahi. Asli paper mein examiner jaan boojh kar lamba distractor bhi daal sakta hai, is liye concept par bharosa karein, length par nahi. In 28 sawalon ko dhyaan se parhein: yehi woh jagahein hain jahan sirf guess karne wala student fail hota hai.
